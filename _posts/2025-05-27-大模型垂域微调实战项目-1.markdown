### 解决

github上有issue解决了：(https://github.com/huggingface/transformers/issues/36298) 降低bitsandbytes即可

```
pip uninstall bitsandbytes
pip installl bitsandbytes==0.44.0 -i https://pypi.tuna.tsinghua.edu.cn/simple
```

**成功！**
